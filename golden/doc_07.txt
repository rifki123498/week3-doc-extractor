PT Edukasi Masa Depan provides online professional training and certification programs for Indonesian workers.

The company generated Rp48 billion of revenue and Rp6 billion of EBITDA during 2025.

It is seeking Rp15 billion in Series A financing to develop new courses and expand its corporate customer base.

The company is led by CEO Putri Rahman and CTO Daniel Hartono.
