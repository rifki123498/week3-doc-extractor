PT Sehat Bersama is an Indonesian healthcare services company operating outpatient clinics in Jakarta and Bandung.

The company generated Rp72 billion in revenue in 2025.

Management is seeking Rp18 billion in Series A funding to open additional clinics and strengthen its digital patient platform.

The CEO is Dr. Rafi Mahendra and the COO is Lina Kusuma.

Key risks include doctor retention, regulatory compliance, and slow ramp-up of new clinic locations.

The company did not disclose EBITDA in the investment summary.
