PT Garuda Digital operates a B2B software platform serving small and medium-sized enterprises in Indonesia.

In 2025, the company recorded Rp90 billion in revenue and Rp10 billion in EBITDA.

Management is seeking Rp25 billion in Series A capital to accelerate product development and expand its sales team.

The business is led by CEO Kevin Tan and CTO Rina Hartono.

Key investment risks include high customer churn, dependence on enterprise renewals, and increasing competition from regional SaaS providers.
