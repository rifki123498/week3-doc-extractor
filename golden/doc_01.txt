PT Merah Putih Foods is an Indonesian food and beverage company
focused on packaged consumer products.

The company generated Rp150 billion in revenue and Rp18 billion
in EBITDA in 2025.

The company is currently raising Rp40 billion in Series B funding
to expand its distribution network across Indonesia.

The CEO is Arif Pratama and the CFO is Sarah Wijaya.

Key risks include dependency on a small number of distributors,
increasing raw material costs, and competition from larger
consumer brands.
