Sinar Energi is building commercial rooftop solar projects across Indonesia.

FY25 sales came in at around IDR 115bn, while EBITDA was approximately IDR 14bn. The business is looking for IDR 35bn of new capital, although management has not specified a formal funding round.

Rizky Aditya runs the business as CEO. Finance is led by CFO Clara Lim.

Investors highlighted three main concerns: reliance on government regulation, project execution delays, and exposure to imported equipment prices.
