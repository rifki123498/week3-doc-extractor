PT Nusantara Logistics provides last-mile delivery and warehousing services to consumer and e-commerce companies across Java.

Revenue reached Rp210 billion in 2025, with EBITDA of Rp22 billion.

The company is currently seeking Rp50 billion of growth capital to increase warehouse capacity and expand its delivery fleet.

No specific funding round or stage was stated in the document.

The business is managed by CEO Dimas Prakoso and CFO Anita Sari.

Key risks include fuel price volatility, customer concentration, and increasing fleet maintenance costs.
