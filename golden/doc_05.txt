PT Bumi Retail operates a chain of specialty retail stores focused on lifestyle and household products.

In 2025, the company reported Rp320 billion in revenue and Rp38 billion in EBITDA.

The company is raising Rp75 billion in Series C funding to open new stores, improve its supply chain systems, and invest in e-commerce.

The management team consists of:
- Andre Wijaya, Chief Executive Officer
- Melisa Gunawan, Chief Financial Officer
- Fajar Hidayat, Chief Operating Officer
- Nadia Putri, Chief Commercial Officer

Key risks include aggressive store expansion, inventory obsolescence, lease cost inflation, and weaker consumer spending.
